## Reference research

- Apple iPhone Air official product page: https://www.apple.com/iphone-air/ — inspected its product imagery, hierarchy and film entry. Full film playback was not verified; no frame-by-frame movie analysis is claimed.
- Shopee Malaysia's official Warong Pak Oren film: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fo9Mr0QpaD8 — official listing and player inspected; browser access did not remain available for complete playback. Its film length/content is not being copied.
- Lazada Always Better, creative director's case study: https://www.shuyau.com/work/always-better — inspected published campaign explanation and embedded-film entries; complete playback was not verified. Emphasis on a concrete human situation is the design interpretation used here, not a claim to reproduce its campaign.

No Apple, Shopee or Lazada footage, logos or music are included as RainReady assets. The proposed direction combines restrained product detail with an original local setting; “Apple-level” is not treated as a measurable guarantee.
